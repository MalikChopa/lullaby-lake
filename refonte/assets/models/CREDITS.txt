Lullaby Lake - 3D models of the HD-2D world (refonte/assets/models/*.glb)
=======================================================================

These models come from the Kenney asset packs listed below.
Created/distributed by Kenney (www.kenney.nl) - license: Creative Commons Zero, CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
Crediting Kenney is not mandatory under CC0; we do it anyway, with thanks.

HOW THEY WERE MODIFIED (by refonte/tools/art-bake.mjs, then at load time by refonte/src/world/models.js):
 - converted to one lean GLB per model: node transforms baked in, colour map / material colours sampled into vertex colours, normals and texture dropped;
 - recoloured at load time to the game palette (hue anchors, light desaturation, warm tint, quantised levels; house walls and roofs recoloured per building);
 - scaled and rotated to the footprints of the game map and merged with the other props of each chunk (one draw call per material family).
No model was redrawn; the game logic, the pixel-art sprites and the textures of the walls, roofs and decks are the project's own.
